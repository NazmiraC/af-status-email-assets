WhatsApp self-service

Delivered

The AFBF WhatsApp channel went live on 20 August 2026 and has supported approximately 2,000 interactions across service requests, document submissions and beneficiary support.

Current focus

Data segmentation analysis is underway to determine the viability of WhatsApp within Corporate Operations. Data mapping and validation activities are also in progress within the health business.

Digital profile
  • A single, trusted client profile will be securely reused across AF Connect, AFI and other Alexforbes platforms.
  • Centralised profile and consent management, standardised verification and event-driven integration will improve data consistency and help keep client information current.
The project is currently in the analysis phase, with no projected go-live date.
  
Verification
  • A reusable verification capability will strengthen controls over high-risk digital transactions, with High-Risk Claims as the first use case.
  • High-Risk Claims will be the first use case to leverage this capability, with the intention of supporting future digital journeys over time, including AF Invest.
The project is currently in the analysis phase, with no projected go-live date.

Overall business value

Together, these initiatives will deliver simpler, safer and more consistent experiences across mobile, WhatsApp and web; expand self-service; strengthen data and fraud controls; and reduce the cost and risk associated with fragmented platforms, processes and information.

This progress strengthens Alexforbes’ ability to scale secure digital services and accelerate future innovation.
